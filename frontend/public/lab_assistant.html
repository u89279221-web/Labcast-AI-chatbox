📜 3D Printer Quick Reference

  • 1. Bed Preparation
    Clean PEI surface with 99% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) before every print.
  • 2. Standard Preheat Temps
    • PLA: Nozzle 200°C | Bed 60°C
    • PETG: Nozzle 240°C | Bed 70°C
    • ABS: Nozzle 250°C | Bed 100°C
  • 3. Hot Burn Hazard
    Never touch nozzle during operation (200°C+). Wear heat-resistant safety gloves.
